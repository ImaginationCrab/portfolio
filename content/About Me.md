I'm flattered that you would take the time out of your day to know more about me! I live to do two things: write code and snowboard. Ever since I was a child I have loved programming, writing mods for my favorite games to building niche projects that have never seen the light of day. I think the first time I touched code was when I was 9, having to write a mod for a game since the specific function I wanted didn't exist at the time. Of course, it didn't work, but I think that was the source of my inspiration. Though, there aren't many photos to share about my hobby of programming.

</div>
</div>
<div class="two-column">

<div class="col-left">

When I was 17, I discovered snowboarding, which is the gallery of photos you see here. Ever since I was first exposed, I have been obsessed with shredding the snow and traveling the world to do so. I have been to many parts of the USA and even international to snowboard. My favorite resort as of right now is probably Copper Mountain in Colorado, I think their park is the nicest. Obviously, I am not the best at snowboarding but I try to go every winter, to catch up to those who started really early. I plan on going back to Colorado, but maybe I will see the mountains in Japan first, who knows?
